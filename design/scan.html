Scan a Railgun wallet

Enter a viewing key and a start date. The scanner decrypts the notes visible to that key and builds a transaction ledger from the start date to the current block.

The viewing key could not be parsed. Check that the full key was copied.

Export it from the Railgun wallet as a shareable viewing key. It grants read access to balances and history. It cannot spend funds.

The scan starts at the first block on or after 00:00 UTC on this date. Transactions before it are not included. If unsure, use the date the wallet was created. Earlier dates take longer to scan.
